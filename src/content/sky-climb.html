What is Sky Climb?

Sky Climb is OpenNoillirk played as a balloon ride. You get the same ten daily prompts as everyone else, 30 seconds for each, while a small krill waits by a forest pond with three balloons tied on. Every answer the game accepts lifts it, and the more common your answer, the higher your krill floats: the board's #1 answer sends it furthest of all. A Daily you play here counts on the home page too.

How the climb works

Points become altitude. Your running total is your height, and nothing ever pulls the krill back down.

  1. 30 seconds a prompt

    Each prompt names a category. Type the answer you think most people would give before the ring around the clock closes. A pass or a timeout scores nothing, and the krill holds its height.

  2. Five bands

    Where your answer sits on our board puts it in one of five bands. Later questions count more: ×2 from question 3, ×3 from question 6, ×4 from question 9.

    • Off the Board10 points
    • Top 1025 points
    • Top 550 points
    • Runner-up75 points
    • #1 Answer100 points

  3. Points become height

    Every point lifts the krill: 100 points take it to 30m, and a perfect Daily of 2,500 points ends at 14.0 km. The ruler on the right counts the height and marks what you pass, from the Eiffel Tower to the top of Everest.

  4. NEXT when you're ready

    Tap the sky to skip the rise. Then the result panel shows your band, your points and the board's top five, and waits: press NEXT, or Enter, for the next prompt.

Challenge a friend to out-climb you

Every Daily ends with Share & challenge. It makes share cards with your height and zone, a short text and a challenge link. The text shows your height, your zone, a coloured circle for each answer and your title, but never the answers themselves.

A friend who opens the link plays the same ten prompts on the climb, with your krill rising beside theirs in its own colour and your name over its head. When yours is out of view, an arrow on their ruler shows your name and height, so they always know how far there is to go. The higher krill wins.

Unlimited runs of 10 or 20 prompts come with a link too. The full rules are on how to play and scoring.

Sky Climb FAQ

Short answers about the balloon stage.

Is Sky Climb a different game from OpenNoillirk?

No. It's the same game on another stage: the same daily prompts, the same 30 seconds, the same board and the same points. Only the picture changes, from a crowd on a tabletop to a krill on balloons.

Does a Daily I play here count on the home page?

Yes. Both pages play today's Daily and keep it in the same place in this browser. Finish it here and the home page shows your result; stop halfway and either page picks up where you left off.

How high can the krill go?

A perfect Daily of 2,500 points reaches 14.0 km, up in the jet stream. A perfect 20-prompt Unlimited run reaches the mesosphere, and an endless run keeps rising past the aurora into low Earth orbit.

Do I need an account or an app?

No. Sky Climb is free and runs in the browser on a phone or a computer, with no sign-up. Your progress, your best heights and your streak are saved in this browser.

Can I play on the tabletop instead?

Yes. The home page has both stages, with a switch on its start screen, and it remembers the one you pick. This page always plays Sky Climb.
