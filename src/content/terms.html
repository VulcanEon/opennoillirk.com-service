Terms of play

Updated October 15, 2026

OpenNoillirk is free to play and needs no account. OpenNoillirk is an independently hosted adaptation using authorized Noillirk assets. It is not affiliated with Daily Games.

Playing the game

Daily offers ten questions for your local date, with thirty seconds each, on Sky Climb or Tabletop. Unlimited offers ten, twenty or endless prompts with a fresh seeded shuffle. An accepted answer ends the round, and a refused answer lets you try again while time remains. Passing or running out of time scores nothing.

Scores, bands and answer lists

Every prompt has a board: our estimate, made in advance, of how 100 people would answer, from the most common answer down to the tenth. An answer’s band comes from its place on that board. Boards are game values, not measured counts or live counts of other players’ answers, which is why the game marks them (est.). Our list of accepted answers may not contain every valid answer. Points, people and titles are for play and personal comparison; they have no cash value and carry no prize.

Challenge links and nicknames

A challenge link shows the nicknames, titles and scores of everyone who played it, and each player’s answers during the game; anyone who has the link can see them. The game gives you a random nickname. If you change it, choose one you are happy for friends to see, and don’t use someone else’s name or an offensive one; the game replaces some offensive nicknames with a random one.

Availability and saved progress

The game, question bank and presentation may change, and a challenge made on an older question set may no longer open as it did. Saved progress belongs to the browser and device you used. Clearing browser data, changing browsers or using private browsing can remove it.

Respect the game and other players

Please don’t disrupt the service, interfere with other people’s access, or present another person’s game or artwork as your own. You are welcome to share your own results and send challenge links to friends.

Changes

We may update these terms as the game changes. The date at the top identifies this version. Read our privacy notice for how saved progress, game statistics and sharing work.
